## Short Answers
- Number theory studies the properties of integers and their relationships.
- GCD is the largest positive divisor common to two numbers.
- Modular arithmetic works with remainders after division.
- RSA uses the fact that multiplying large primes is easy, while factoring the product is hard.
- The public key is shared and the private key is kept secret.
